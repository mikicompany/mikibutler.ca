Office Mural

November 1, 2021 · 1 min read
muraloffworld industriestraditional artprocess

Last October, I was commissioned by Offworld Industries to design and paint a mural for the studio. The entire wall is approximately 10 × 3 m — here are some photos and video from the process. \ :) /

I painted it over five days, using a mix of latex-based paint, paint dabbers, some aerosol, and good old paintbrushes — together with a lot of coffee.

Thank you to everyone who contributed to the wall. You guys are the best <3
