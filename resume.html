Hi, I'm Miki Butler — a Senior Environment Artist based in Vancouver, Canada, with 18 years crafting immersive game worlds. Making games is what makes me happy, and happy is good. :)

I've shipped titles across PC, PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo, and mobile — for studios including Capcom, EA, Ubisoft, Offworld Industries, and Next Level Games — working in Unreal Engine, Unity, Frostbite, and a range of proprietary tech. From Starship Troopers Extermination to Dead Rising 4 and Plants vs Zombies, I specialize in environment and prop art across a wide range of styles and teams.
