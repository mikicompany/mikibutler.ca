Pocket Sculpt — a free 3D sculpting tool

Pocket Sculpt is a free, no-download 3D sculpting tool that runs right in your browser. Push digital clay around with draw, smooth, inflate, grab and flatten brushes, work symmetrically with mirror X, try 13 materials, then render and share a PNG. It installs to your home screen and works offline. Made by Miki Butler, a 3D environment artist.

  • Drag on the clay to sculpt; drag empty space to orbit; pinch to zoom and pan.
  • Keys: 1–5 pick a brush, X toggles mirror, Ctrl/⌘+Z undo.
