Copied! Now paste into Gmail.
  1. In Gmail: ⚙ Settings → See all settings → General → Signature.
  2. Select your old (broken) signature in the box and delete it.
  3. Click Copy signature above, then paste (Ctrl/Cmd + V) into the box.
  4. Scroll down and Save Changes.
